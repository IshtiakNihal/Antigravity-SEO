# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As founders, maintainers, and contributors to **Antigravity SEO**, we pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of technical experience.

---

## Standards of Behavior

### We Encourage:
* Constructive, evidence-based feedback and technical collaboration.
* Respect for differing approaches to search engine optimization and agentic workflows.
* Clear, transparent communication and helping newcomers get started.

### Unacceptable Behavior:
* Harassment, derogatory comments, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
* Public or private harassment of any community member.
* Publishing others' private information without consent.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Community leadership, headed by project founder **Ahmed Ishtiak Nihal**, is committed to upholding these standards fairly. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately via a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/IshtiakNihal/Antigravity-SEO/security/advisories/new).
