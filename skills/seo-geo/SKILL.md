# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Skill

Generative AI search engines extract direct factual entities from websites to answer conversational queries. This skill provides the scoring and formatting required to win AI citations.

## The GEO Scoring Formula (0–100)

* **Entity Density (35%):** Specific verifiable physical metrics (battery mAh, wattage, motor RPM, codecs, dimensions, materials).
* **Data Structure (25%):** Clean 2-column HTML `<table>` layouts (`.mjt-spec-table`) which LLMs prioritize for comparative analysis.
* **Anti-AI Natural Tone (20%):** Zero robotic fluff words (*"revolutionary"*, *"game-changer"*, *"delve into"*, *"testament to"*).
* **Brand & Authority Anchor (20%):** Strong entity mapping between manufacturer and specific model numbers.
