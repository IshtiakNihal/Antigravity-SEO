# Ecommerce SEO & Catalog Integrity Skill

This sub-skill ensures that ecommerce product pages not only rank on Google and AI search engines but also convert visitors into paying customers.

## Critical Catalog Acceptance Criteria

1. **Price Integrity:** No product may have a price of `0` or empty string. Zero-price products break checkout progression and trigger automatic Google Merchant Center suspensions.
2. **SKU Standardization:** Every simple product and every variation child must possess a standardized, unique SKU (`MJ-[ID]` or `MJ-[Parent]-[Option]`).
3. **Title Hygiene:** Keep the core CMS product title clean of repetitive phrases like `"Price in Bangladesh"`. Let the SEO `<title>` tag handle local buying intent.
4. **Summary & Excerpt Discipline:** Keep the top excerpt compact (3–5 bullet points). Detailed specification tables belong in the main product content tab to avoid breaking mobile layouts.
5. **Authentic Warranty Badges:** Clearly distinguish official brand warranties (e.g. 1 Year Official Warranty) from standard store replacement guarantees.
