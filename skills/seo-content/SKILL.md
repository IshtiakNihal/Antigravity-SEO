# Content Quality & Anti-AI Humanizer

Ensures content meets Google's People-First content guidelines and avoids algorithmic devaluation from AI detection filters.

## Anti-AI Fluff Word Blacklist
The following phrases must be flagged and removed from customer-facing copy:
* *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."*
* *"A testament to..."*
* *"Revolutionary game-changer"*
* *"Delve into..."*
* *"Seamlessly blends form and function"*
* *"Look no further than..."*
* *"In conclusion..."*
