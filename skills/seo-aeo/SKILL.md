# Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Skill

Google's AI Overviews and featured snippets look for concise, direct answers to conversational questions. This skill structures question-and-answer content for maximum visibility.

## The 10 FAQ Standard
Every entity, product, or buying guide must feature 10 distinct, model-specific FAQs covering:
1. Real-world capability & waterproofing
2. OS & device compatibility
3. Battery life & fast charging
4. In the box package contents
5. Official warranty duration and terms
6. Cleaning and maintenance
7. Cash on Delivery & arrival inspection
8. Differentiator vs earlier models
9. Skin safety & durability
10. Defective arrival replacement policy

## Upfront Direct Answer Architecture
Every answer begins with an immediate, factual 1–2 sentence answer before explaining technical details.
